THE ANTONIO JEFFERSON STUDIO

The studio is a bright, 1100 sq ft creative space with soaring 15' ceilings and stunning natural light pouring in from oversized windows with amazing Manhattan views, including the Manhattan Bridge. Located on the 7th floor of a newly renovated building with three elevators and 24/7 access, the space includes access to men's and women's restrooms, AC/heat, and a freight elevator.

Our studio is perfect for photoshoots, filming, interviews, pop-ups, art exhibits, classes, castings, rehearsals, events, and more.
Located in the heart of DUMBO—one of NYC's most inspiring neighborhoods—just one stop from Manhattan. Surrounded by the best restaurants, bars, attractions, and shops, and easily accessible from nearly anywhere.
Within walking distance, you'll find: TimeOut Market, KITH, DUMBO House, Brooklyn Bridge, Jane's Carousel, The River Café, Juliana's, and Nobody Told Me.

Whether you're building content, showcasing art, or hosting a community event—we've got the space, light, and energy to bring your vision to life.
